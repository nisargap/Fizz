How it connects

The secure layer between agents and hardware.

A Raspberry Pi pairs with one command and a short code you approve on the device. Agents like Hermes connect over MCP and ask Fizz, never your devices directly.

Devices

Raspberry PiLinux boards
ESP32Wi-Fi microcontrollers
Any deviceAnything with HTTPS
  1. 01

    Pair Live

    On a Pi, run curl -fsSL https://fizzlayer.com/install.sh | sh, then fizz pair, and approve the request there. ESP32s post to the same webhook with a sensor key.

  2. 02

    Authorize Live

    Every request is checked against that device's key. Rotate or revoke one device without touching the rest.

  3. 03

    Connect agents Live

    Hermes, Claude Code, and other MCP clients connect with their own revocable token. They read sensors and pair devices, and never see device keys.

Hermes Agent is an open-source project by Nous Research. Muse is a product of Meta. Fizz is not affiliated with either. Agents connect through Fizz's MCP server with a bearer token; Muse support depends on the connector options Meta offers.

Use cases

Watch the places you can't.

Give Fizz a sensor and a limit. Ask about it in plain English, and get a phone call when something goes wrong.

Flow · L/min

Basement leak watch

A flow sensor on the main line. Water moving at 3 AM when nobody is up means a leak, and Fizz calls you while it is still a puddle.

ASK “Was there any water flow overnight?”

ALERT Call me if flow is above 0.5 L/min

Gas · ppm

Garage air check

A second set of eyes beside your detector, near the furnace or workbench. Fizz keeps the history and calls when concentration climbs.

ASK “Did the garage air spike today?”

ALERT Call me if gas is above 1,000 ppm

Temperature · °C

Server closet heat

A probe by the rack. When the AC quits on a Saturday, Fizz calls before your hardware starts to throttle.

ASK “What's the closet temperature right now?”

ALERT Call me if it is above 30 °C

Humidity · %

Greenhouse and grow rooms

Know how your plants are doing before you drive over. Fizz watches the air where they live.

ASK “Is the greenhouse too dry?”

ALERT Call me if humidity drops below 40%

Sound · dB

Nursery and pet room

A sound level sensor in the room. Fizz notices when it gets loud while you are out.

ASK “Has it been quiet in the nursery?”

ALERT Call me if sound is above 70 dB

Phone · no hardware

Your phone as a sensor

Pair any phone with a one-time link to share motion, location, and voice notes, each behind its own permission.

ASK “What did my phone say?”

CONTROL Revoke the phone from either device

Also: pressure, radio signal strength, and custom metrics from any device that can send an HTTPS request.

Enterprise

Every sensor on site. One agent watching.

Warehouses, fleets, and factory floors already run on sensors. Fizz gives them one place to ask questions, and calls the right person when a reading crosses the line.

Temperature · humidity · water

Warehouses and cold storage

Probes in every cold room, humidity on the racks, and leak sensors at the dock. Fizz watches every zone and calls the shift lead before stock spoils.

ASK “Which cold room is warmest right now?”

ALERT Call the shift lead if a cold room is above 5 °C

Phone · temperature · API

Logistics and fleets

A driver's phone shares location and motion, and a reefer probe posts temperatures over HTTPS. Each device has its own key, so one truck can be cut off without touching the rest.

ASK “Is truck 12's reefer still under 4 °C?”

ALERT Call dispatch if a reefer is above 4 °C

Pressure · sound · gas

Factories and plants

Boiler pressure, compressor noise, and air quality on the line. Check the floor from anywhere, and get a call when a machine drifts out of range.

ASK “What's boiler 2 pressure right now?”

ALERT Call maintenance if pressure is above 900 kPa
